# HifzMate Account Deletion Request

Users may request deletion of their HifzMate account and associated data by emailing:

hifzmate.support@gmail.com

Please include:

- Registered email address
- Subject: Account Deletion Request

Upon verification, we will delete:

- Account information
- Memorization progress
- Mistake records
- Bookmarks
- Revision history
- Associated user data

Deletion requests are typically processed within 30 days.
