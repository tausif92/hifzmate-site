# Privacy Policy for HifzMate

**Last Updated:** July 2026

## Introduction

HifzMate ("we", "our", or "us") is a Quran memorization and revision application designed to help users track their Hifz progress, review mistakes, strengthen retention, and improve their Quran memorization journey.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

By using HifzMate, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

---

## Information We Collect

When you create an account, we may collect:

- Email address
- Authentication information required to sign in
- User account identifiers

### User Content and Progress

To provide Hifz tracking and revision features, we may store:

- Memorization progress
- Revision history
- Mistake records
- Bookmarks
- Last-read position
- User preferences and settings
- App usage related to Quran memorization activities

### Technical Information

We may automatically collect limited technical information necessary for operating and improving the application, including:

- Device type
- Operating system version
- App version
- Diagnostic and error information
- Performance-related information
